The Revenge Manifesto

Why I build
like this

  1. Downtime is a bug in my character, not just my stack.Every outage is a promise broken to a real human on the other side of the screen. I take that personally — professionally, but personally.
  2. Free tools should be first-class tools.World-class reliability engineering shouldn't be gated behind enterprise invoices. Open source is the great equalizer.
  3. Self-hosting is sovereignty.Your monitoring data is operational intelligence. It belongs on your infrastructure, not someone's dashboard.
  4. Alert fatigue is enemy fire.90+ channels exist so the right signal reaches the right human — not so every phone in the room screams at once.
